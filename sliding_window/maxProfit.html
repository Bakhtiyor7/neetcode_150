The pattern

left only ever moves forward, onto right

Plot the day each pointer sits on, loop by loop. right climbs one day per loop. left stays flat until a cheaper day shows up, then it jumps up to meet right.

The window is every day from left to right. When left jumps, the days behind it are dropped for good. Because right moves forward by one each loop, each day is visited once.
